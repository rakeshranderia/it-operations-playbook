# IT Governance Model

## Overview

IT governance provides the structure through which technology decisions are aligned with business strategy, risk appetite and organisational priorities.

Effective governance should create clarity around decision-making, accountability, investment and risk without unnecessarily slowing delivery.

---

## Governance Principles

Technology governance should be:

- Business aligned
- Risk based
- Transparent
- Accountable
- Proportionate
- Outcome focused
- Adaptable

---

## Key Governance Domains

### Strategy

Ensure technology investment supports organisational strategy.

Consider:

- Business priorities
- Technology strategy
- Digital transformation
- Architecture
- Future capability

### Risk

Understand and manage technology-related risk.

Consider:

- Cybersecurity
- Information security
- Technology resilience
- Third-party risk
- Data risk
- Regulatory obligations

### Investment

Ensure technology investment delivers appropriate value.

Consider:

- Business case
- Total cost of ownership
- Strategic alignment
- Risk reduction
- Expected benefits
- Lifecycle costs

### Architecture

Maintain alignment between technology platforms and the target operating model.

Consider:

- Standards
- Technology lifecycle
- Integration
- Security
- Scalability
- Technical debt

### Performance

Monitor whether technology services and investments are delivering expected outcomes.

Consider:

- Service performance
- Project delivery
- Financial performance
- Risk
- Customer experience
- Business outcomes

---

## Decision Rights

Technology governance should establish clear decision rights.

| Decision | Typical Accountability |
|---|---|
| Technology Strategy | CIO / Executive |
| Technology Investment | Executive / Investment Committee |
| Security Risk | CISO / Risk / Executive |
| Architecture | Technology Leadership |
| Service Performance | Service Owner |
| Vendor Selection | Business + Technology + Procurement |
| Major Technology Change | Change Governance |
| Technology Risk Acceptance | Appropriate Risk Owner |

---

## Three Lines of Accountability

Technology governance should align with the broader risk management model.

### First Line

Technology teams own and manage operational risks.

### Second Line

Risk, compliance and security functions provide oversight, challenge and guidance.

### Third Line

Internal audit provides independent assurance.

---

## Governance vs Management

Governance should focus on:

**Direction + Oversight + Accountability**

Management should focus on:

**Execution + Operations + Delivery**

Confusing the two can result in excessive governance or insufficient oversight.

---

## Executive Reporting

Technology governance reporting should provide visibility of:

- Strategic initiatives
- Major risks
- Technology investment
- Service performance
- Security
- Resilience
- Vendor performance
- Key decisions

---

## Executive Perspective

Good technology governance does not mean more meetings.

It means ensuring that:

> **The right people make the right decisions with the right information at the right time.**

The objective is to create accountability and transparency while allowing technology teams to execute effectively.
