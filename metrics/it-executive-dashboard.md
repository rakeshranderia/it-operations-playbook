# IT Executive Dashboard

## Overview

An effective technology dashboard should provide executives with a concise view of the organisation's technology environment.

The dashboard should focus on:

**Performance + Risk + Resilience + Investment + Business Outcomes**

The objective is to support decision-making rather than simply report activity.

---

## Executive Dashboard Categories

A practical technology dashboard can be structured into six areas:

1. Service Performance
2. Security & Risk
3. Resilience
4. Delivery
5. Financial
6. Strategic Outcomes

---

## 1. Service Performance

Measures the reliability and quality of technology services.

Examples:

- Critical service availability
- Major incidents
- Incident volume
- Incident resolution time
- Service request performance
- SLA achievement
- User satisfaction

### Example KPIs

**Critical Service Availability**

Percentage availability of critical business services.

**Major Incidents**

Number of significant technology incidents during the reporting period.

---

## 2. Security & Technology Risk

Provides visibility of material technology risk.

Examples:

- Critical vulnerabilities
- Security incidents
- High-risk findings
- Outstanding remediation
- Technology risk exceptions
- Third-party risks
- Compliance status

Metrics should focus on material exposure rather than simply reporting the number of security events.

---

## 3. Resilience

Measures the organisation's ability to withstand and recover from disruption.

Examples:

- Critical services with tested recovery plans
- DR test results
- Backup success
- Recovery capability
- Outstanding resilience issues
- Critical supplier dependencies

### Example KPI

**Critical Service Recovery Coverage**

Percentage of critical services with tested and validated recovery capability.

---

## 4. Delivery

Measures execution of the technology roadmap.

Examples:

- Projects on track
- Projects at risk
- Budget variance
- Milestone achievement
- Benefits realised
- Major dependencies
- Resource capacity

A project should not be considered successful simply because it was delivered on time.

The outcome should also be measured.

---

## 5. Financial

Provides visibility of technology expenditure and investment.

Examples:

- Actual vs budget
- Forecast
- Run vs change expenditure
- Vendor spend
- Cloud spend
- Licensing
- Major investments
- Benefits realised

---

## 6. Strategic Outcomes

Measures whether technology is delivering business value.

Examples:

- Productivity improvements
- Automation
- Customer experience
- Digital adoption
- AI adoption
- Revenue enablement
- Cost reduction
- Risk reduction

---

---

## RAG Status

A simple RAG model can provide rapid executive visibility.

### Green

Operating within agreed tolerance.

### Amber

Requires attention or management action.

### Red

Outside agreed tolerance or requires executive intervention.

RAG status should always be supported by a clear explanation and action.

---

## Executive Commentary

Dashboards should not rely solely on numbers.

Each material Amber or Red item should explain:

**What happened?**

**Why does it matter?**

**What are we doing about it?**

**When will it be resolved?**

**What decision or support is required?**

---

## Board-Level Reporting

Board reporting should focus on material issues and trends.

The board generally does not need detailed operational information.

It should understand:

- Material technology risks
- Significant incidents
- Resilience
- Strategic technology investments
- Major transformation
- Cybersecurity
- Regulatory obligations
- Emerging technology risks
- Business outcomes

---

## Trend Analysis

A single month's result may not provide meaningful insight.

Executive reporting should identify trends such as:

- Improving
- Deteriorating
- Stable
- Emerging risk
- Recurring issue

Trend information allows executives to distinguish between isolated events and systemic problems.

---

## Executive Perspective

A strong technology dashboard should allow leadership to understand the state of technology within minutes.

The objective is to answer:

> **Is technology performing?**

> **Are we secure and resilient?**

> **Are we delivering our strategy?**

> **Are we managing investment effectively?**

> **Are we creating business value?**

The best executive dashboard is not the one with the most metrics.

It is the one that enables the **right decisions to be made quickly.**
