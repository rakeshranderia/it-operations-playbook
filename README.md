# IT Operations Playbook

A practical executive framework for operating, governing and continuously improving an IT function.

## Purpose

This repository explores the principles, processes and governance practices required to operate an effective modern IT function.

The focus is on aligning technology operations with business objectives, risk appetite and organisational priorities.

---

## Focus Areas

- IT Operating Model
- Service Management
- Technology Governance
- Vendor Management
- Business Continuity
- Disaster Recovery
- Technology Risk
- IT Financial Management
- Capacity and Resource Management
- Technology Roadmaps
- Executive Reporting

---

## Operating Model

The approach is based on:

**Strategy → Governance → Delivery → Operations → Measurement → Improvement**

---

## Executive Perspective

A modern IT function should be more than a technology support service.

It should provide:

**Reliable Operations + Effective Governance + Business Enablement + Continuous Improvement**

The objective is to create an IT capability that is secure, resilient, measurable and aligned with business strategy.

---

## Status

This repository represents a developing executive framework.

Future versions will incorporate practical examples, implementation experience and lessons learned from real-world technology operations.
