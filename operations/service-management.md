# IT Service Management

## Overview

IT Service Management (ITSM) provides the processes and practices required to deliver reliable, supportable and measurable technology services.

The objective is not simply to resolve tickets.

Effective ITSM should support:

**Availability + Reliability + User Experience + Risk Management + Continuous Improvement**

---

## Core ITSM Capabilities

A mature IT service management environment typically includes:

- Incident Management
- Service Request Management
- Problem Management
- Change Management
- Knowledge Management
- Configuration Management
- Service Level Management
- Service Continuity
- Continual Improvement

---

## Incident Management

### Objective

Restore normal service as quickly as practical while minimising business impact.

The process should include:

**Detect → Log → Categorise → Prioritise → Resolve → Validate → Close**

Priority should consider both:

**Business Impact + Urgency**

---

## Major Incident Management

Major incidents require an enhanced response model.

Key components include:

- Clear incident ownership
- Technical response team
- Business stakeholders
- Executive escalation
- Communications
- Decision-making
- Recovery coordination
- Post-incident review

Executive communications should focus on:

**Impact → Current Situation → Actions → Expected Recovery → Decisions Required**

---

## Problem Management

Incident management restores service.

Problem management seeks to understand and address the underlying cause.

A practical process is:

**Identify → Analyse → Root Cause → Remediate → Verify → Learn**

Problem management should focus on recurring or significant incidents.

---

## Change Management

Changes should be managed according to their potential risk and impact.

### Standard Change

Low-risk, repeatable and pre-approved.

### Normal Change

Requires assessment and appropriate approval.

### Emergency Change

Required to address an urgent business or technology risk.

The objective is not to prevent change.

It is to:

> **Make change predictable and reduce unnecessary operational risk.**

---

## Service Requests

Common service requests include:

- Access requests
- Hardware
- Software
- New accounts
- Permissions
- Standard configuration changes

Service requests should be standardised and automated where practical.

---

## Service Levels

Service levels should reflect business requirements.

Examples include:

- Availability
- Response time
- Resolution time
- Request fulfilment
- Support hours
- Recovery objectives

Service levels should be measurable and meaningful rather than simply tracking activity.

---

## Knowledge Management

A strong knowledge base can reduce support demand and improve resolution times.

Knowledge may include:

- User guides
- Technical procedures
- Troubleshooting
- Known errors
- FAQs
- Recovery procedures

Knowledge should be reviewed and updated as services evolve.

---

## Configuration Management

Understanding technology dependencies is critical for effective operations.

A configuration management capability should provide visibility of:

- Applications
- Infrastructure
- Devices
- Services
- Vendors
- Dependencies
- Ownership

This information can support:

- Incident management
- Change management
- Security
- Risk
- Business continuity

---

## Automation

ITSM processes should be automated where practical.

Examples include:

- Password resets
- User provisioning
- Access requests
- Software deployment
- Ticket routing
- Notifications
- Monitoring
- Reporting

Automation should reduce repetitive work while maintaining appropriate controls.

---

## Continual Improvement

Service management should use operational data to identify opportunities for improvement.

Consider:

- Recurring incidents
- Service performance
- User feedback
- Automation opportunities
- Technology lifecycle
- Cost
- Risk

---

## Executive Perspective

A mature ITSM capability should move the organisation from:

**Reactive → Controlled → Measured → Proactive**

The objective is not to maximise the number of tickets closed.

It is to provide **reliable technology services that support business outcomes.**
