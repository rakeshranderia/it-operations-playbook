# IT Operating Model

## Overview

An effective IT operating model defines how technology supports the organisation, how responsibilities are assigned and how technology services are delivered.

The operating model should align technology capability with business strategy, risk appetite and organisational priorities.

---

## Operating Model Principles

A practical IT operating model should be:

- Business aligned
- Service oriented
- Risk aware
- Scalable
- Measurable
- Resilient
- Continuously improving

---

## Core IT Capabilities

A modern IT function typically requires capabilities across:

### Strategy

- Technology strategy
- Enterprise architecture
- Technology roadmap
- Investment planning

### Governance

- Technology risk
- Security
- Compliance
- Policy
- Architecture governance
- Vendor governance

### Delivery

- Project delivery
- Change management
- Application delivery
- Technology transformation

### Operations

- Service management
- Infrastructure
- Applications
- End-user computing
- Monitoring
- Incident management

### Enablement

- Automation
- Data
- AI
- Digital platforms
- Business process improvement

---

## Service Ownership

Technology services should have clear ownership.

A practical model is:

**Business Owner + Technology Owner + Service Provider**

This creates accountability across business outcomes, technology delivery and operational performance.
