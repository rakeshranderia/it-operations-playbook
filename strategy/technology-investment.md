# Technology Investment & Financial Management

## Overview

Technology investment should be managed as a business portfolio rather than simply as an IT expense.

Effective technology financial management provides visibility of:

- What the organisation is spending
- Where it is spending
- Why the investment is required
- What value is expected
- What risks are being reduced
- Whether the investment is delivering its intended outcome

---

## Technology Cost Categories

A practical technology financial model can separate expenditure into:

### Run

Costs required to maintain existing services.

Examples:

- Software licensing
- Infrastructure
- Support
- Managed services
- Telecommunications
- Cloud consumption

### Improve

Investment that improves existing capabilities.

Examples:

- Automation
- Process improvement
- Platform upgrades
- Service improvements
- Integration

### Transform

Investment that creates new business capabilities.

Examples:

- New platforms
- Digital transformation
- AI
- Data platforms
- Major system replacement

### Protect

Investment that reduces risk.

Examples:

- Cybersecurity
- Resilience
- Disaster recovery
- Identity
- Compliance
- Technology lifecycle

---

## Business Case

Technology investments should have a clear business case.

A practical business case should address:

1. Problem or opportunity
2. Strategic alignment
3. Expected outcomes
4. Options considered
5. Cost
6. Risk
7. Dependencies
8. Implementation approach
9. Benefits
10. Measurement
